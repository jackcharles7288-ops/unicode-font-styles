Fancy Letters A to Z

The full copy and paste chart: every letter, in 15 fancy styles.

Click any letter to copy it, then paste it into an Instagram bio, a Discord or gaming name, a TikTok caption, or anywhere else plain text goes. Each section also has a one-click button that copies the whole alphabet in that style.

These are not fonts. Every fancy letter below is a separate Unicode character that happens to look styled, which is why it survives copy and paste into apps that do not allow real fonts. One honest warning: screen readers read many of these characters by their long Unicode names, so treat them as decoration, never as the only copy of something important.

Want whole words instead of single letters? The free generator at Fonti turns any name, word or sentence into 350+ styles in one go, including combinations no chart can show. And if you like mixing styles letter by letter, the companion Unlimited Styles demo lets you build your own.
